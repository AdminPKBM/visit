What an LMS Is and When You Need One

By Kang Ruli · Updated 2026

A learning management system, or LMS, is software that delivers courses, tracks progress, and manages learners. Schools, companies, and course creators use LMS platforms to organize training at scale.

What an LMS usually includes

  • Course structure for modules, lessons, and resources.
  • Enrollment and login for learners.
  • Progress tracking and completion certificates.
  • Assessments such as quizzes and assignments.
  • Reporting for instructors or managers.

When you need one

An LMS becomes useful when you have many learners, several courses, or a need to track completion. If you run one short course for a small group, a simple course page or shared folder may be enough.

How to compare options

Focus on the learners' experience, the cost per active learner, export options for your content, and whether the system integrates with payment and email tools you already use.

Feature sets change often, so verify each platform's current capabilities before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

Is an LMS the same as a course platform?

They overlap. Many course platforms include LMS features, while larger LMS products often target schools and companies with more complex needs.
