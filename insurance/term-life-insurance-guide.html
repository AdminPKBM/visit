How Term Life Insurance Works: A Beginner's Guide

Oleh Kang Ruli · Diperbarui 2026

Catatan: Artikel ini bersifat edukatif dan informasi umum. Ini bukan nasihat keuangan atau asuransi. Keputusan membeli polis sebaiknya diambil setelah berkonsultasi dengan agen berlisensi atau penasihat keuangan yang Anda percaya.

What term life insurance is

Term life insurance provides a death benefit for a set period, called the term. If the insured person dies during that term and the policy is active, the beneficiary receives the agreed payout. If the term ends and the policy is not renewed or converted, coverage stops and there is usually no payout. This simplicity is why term policies are often described as the most straightforward type of life insurance.

What affects the premium

Insurers price policies based on risk. The main factors usually include:

  • Age at the time of application. Premiums generally rise as the applicant gets older.
  • Health, including medical history, current conditions, and sometimes lifestyle habits such as smoking.
  • Term length. Longer terms usually cost more per year than shorter ones, because the insurer carries risk for more years.
  • Coverage amount, which is the death benefit you choose.
  • Occupation and hobbies that may be considered higher risk.

Because underwriting rules vary by insurer and by country, compare quotes from more than one provider using the same coverage amount and term length.

Term life compared with permanent life

Permanent policies, such as whole life or universal life, include a lifelong coverage period and often a savings or cash value component. They cost more and are more complex. Term policies focus only on protection. Many people buy term coverage to match a specific need, such as paying off a mortgage or supporting children until they are financially independent.

Questions to ask before you buy

  1. What is the exact coverage period, and what happens when it ends?
  2. Is the premium guaranteed for the whole term, or can it increase?
  3. What exclusions apply, such as in the first years of a policy or for certain causes of death?
  4. What documents will the beneficiary need to file a claim?
  5. Does the policy include a conversion option to a permanent policy, and at what cost?
  6. How does the insurer handle claims, and what does public complaint data show?

Where to verify information

Check the insurer's official policy document, not only the brochure or summary. In the United States, the National Association of Insurance Commissioners publishes consumer guides, and each state insurance department oversees licensing and complaints. In Indonesia, verify provider licensing through the official financial services regulator. Always confirm current rules and details directly with these sources.

Frequently asked questions

Is term life insurance worth it?

It can be, when you have dependents or debts that would be hard to cover without your income. Whether it fits your situation depends on your full financial picture, which a licensed advisor can help you review.

Can I cancel a term policy?

Most policies can be cancelled, but you may lose the coverage, and premiums already paid are usually not refunded. Read the cancellation terms first.
